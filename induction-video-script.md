# Miraj Media Induction Portal — Walkthrough Video Script

**Total runtime target:** ~2:30–3:00 min
**Format:** Screen recording (silent) + AI voiceover added afterward, synced to the recording

---

## How to record the screen capture

1. Use the **live GitHub Pages URL**, not Live Server — cleaner for a demo, no dev tools/localhost visible.
2. Free screen recorders that work well for this:
   - **Mac:** Cmd+Shift+5 (built-in, no install needed)
   - **QuickTime Player** → File → New Screen Recording
   - **Loom** (free tier, also gives you a shareable link automatically)
3. Record in **1080p**, browser window maximized, no other tabs/bookmarks bar visible if possible (Cmd+Shift+B in Chrome hides the bookmarks bar).
4. Record **silently** — don't worry about talking while clicking. Just move at a natural, slightly slower-than-normal pace so each screen is on-camera for 3–5 seconds before you click, since narration will be added on top afterward and needs breathing room.
5. Do one full real run-through: event selection → details → all 18 topics (or speed through, see note below) → signature → certificate.

**Note on the 18 topics:** if you play through every single topic on camera the video will run long (18 topics × ~15–20 sec each = 5–6 min just for that section). Recommend recording **3–4 representative topics in full** (e.g. PPE, Working at Height, Use of Force, Emergency/Evacuation) to show the format, then either cut/speed up the rest in editing, or just narrate over a fast-forwarded section showing topics flicking past.

## Script

### Scene 1 — Home page (0:00–0:15)
*[Show: index.html hero, buttons, trust strip]*

> "This is the Miraj Media Safety Induction Portal — a digital induction system built for events across the Gulf region and beyond. It covers eighteen safety and security topics, works in four languages, and issues a verified digital certificate at the end."

### Scene 2 — Event selection (0:15–0:30)
*[Show: clicking Individual Induction, event cards with colour + logo]*

> "Every event gets its own listing here, each branded with that event's own colour and logo. The attendee just selects the event they're at."

### Scene 3 — Details form (0:30–0:50)
*[Show: attending-as options including Contractor, filling in name/ID/company/etc.]*

> "Next, a short registration — who they're attending as, whether that's a contractor, service provider, or client staff, their company, and their basic ID details. Takes under a minute."

### Scene 4 — Topics & quiz (0:50–1:40)
*[Show: 3–4 topics playing through, correct/incorrect answer flash, progress dots advancing]*

> "Then the induction itself — eighteen topics, covering everything from PPE and manual handling through to crowd management and emergency response. Each topic ends with a quick visual question to confirm understanding before moving on. Get it wrong, and it won't let you skip ahead."

### Scene 5 — Signature (1:40–1:55)
*[Show: signing on the pad, clicking Submit]*

> "Once every topic's complete, the attendee signs directly on screen to confirm they've done the induction."

### Scene 6 — Certificate (1:55–2:20)
*[Show: certificate appearing with event branding, QR code, cert number]*

> "And that's it — a certificate is generated instantly, branded to that event, with a unique certificate number and a QR code for verification."

### Scene 7 — Verification (2:20–2:35) *(optional, if you record it)*
*[Show: scanning the QR / opening verify.html, valid result showing]*

> "Anyone — site security, the client, an auditor — can scan that QR code and instantly confirm the certificate is genuine, without needing to log into anything."

### Scene 8 — Close (2:35–2:45)
*[Show: back on the home page, or just hold on the certificate]*

> "Simple for the attendee, verifiable for everyone else, and built to handle any event, in any language, at scale."

## Notes for whoever edits it together
- Keep narration slightly behind the visuals — let each screen settle for a beat before the voiceover describes it, rather than talking over the click itself.
- If using Loom, its built-in AI can auto-generate a transcript/captions afterward too — useful for accessibility.
